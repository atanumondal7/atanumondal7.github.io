Design

Verification

  • Environments with drivers, sequencers, monitors, a scoreboard and coverage. I earned Siemens' SystemVerilog UVM Comprehensive Pathway badge by passing three exams.

    Used in UART

  • Generators, drivers, monitors and scoreboards written as classes, including the clocking-block timing traps that come with them.

    Used in ALU, shift register, alarm clock

  • Randomized stimulus that reaches cases a directed test would miss. On simulators without randomize(), I write the randomization by hand.

    Used in ALU, UART, shift register

  • Scoreboards that compare what the design does against a reference model, so a run reports pass or fail by itself.

    Used in UART, ALU

  • Coverage bins and crosses that show which behavior has been exercised. The ALU testbench reached 100% functional coverage.

    Used in ALU, UART, shift register
