uart.svVerified in loopback

UART TX/RX with a UVM testbench

A parameterized UART transmitter and receiver, with a receiver that samples at 16 times the baud rate. A UVM environment drives the transmitter, and a scoreboard checks the receiver's output against a reference model.

The simulator I use, Questa Starter Edition, has no randomize() or covergroup, so the stimulus uses $urandom_range and the coverage is counted by hand in a uvm_subscriber.

usr_tbComplete

Universal shift register testbench

A class-based testbench for a universal shift register. The interesting part was a two-cycle sampling latency caused by clocking-block skew. I fixed it by priming the monitor's edge and adding a two-stage input history buffer.

alarm_clockIn progress

Digital alarm clock

A digital alarm clock built from a spec sheet: seven RTL modules, a run script, and a class-based verification environment with a generator, driver, monitor, scoreboard and coverage.

Latching the alarm time is the open bug, a timing conflict with the controller. The testbench's stimulus, checking and coverage are still being filled in.
